Private Coaching & Mentorship FAQs

Common questions on booking 1-on-1 sparring sessions, game evaluations, and personalized training timetables.

Can I book a 1-on-1 session directly with GM Alexander Ivanov?
Yes, Head Coach Alexander Ivanov takes on select students rated 1400+ for private opening preparation and tournament diagnostic work. Sessions are scheduled through the coaching office based on availability.
Are private lessons conducted online or in person?
Both! You can book in-person private sessions at our Manhattan chess center or join high-definition live interactive sessions with shared analysis boards, voice call, and annotated PGN downloads.
What happens if I need to reschedule a booked private lesson?
We require 24 hours advance notice for rescheduling. Any session rescheduled before this window is credited back to your student account with no penalty.
Do coaches provide written reports and homework after each lesson?
Yes, following every 1-on-1 session, your coach uploads an annotated PGN file containing the variations analyzed, engine evaluations, and a 15-puzzle homework set directly to your Student Dashboard.
