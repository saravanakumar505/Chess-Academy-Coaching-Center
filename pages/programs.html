Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about batch sizes, tournament prep, coach allocation, and our hybrid learning model.

How do you determine which program level I should start with?
Every new student completes a complimentary 30-minute diagnostic session with an International Master or senior coach. We review recent game PGNs, test tactical pattern recognition, and assess endgame knowledge to recommend the exact batch where you will be challenged and thrive.
What is the student-to-coach ratio in live group batches?
We limit all group batches to a strict maximum of 4 to 6 students. This guarantees personalized board interaction, real-time move validation by the master coach, and ample time for individual question-and-answer drills during every session.
Can I take both online and in-person academy classes?
Yes, our academy offers a fully synchronized hybrid training setup. You can attend in-person classes at our main campus in Manhattan, or join live interactive sessions from anywhere via our custom student portal with interactive digital chess boards.
Do you provide live tournament coaching and game reviews?
Post-game analysis is the cornerstone of rapid chess improvement. When students compete in official weekend FIDE or USCF rated tournaments, our coaches dissect their notation scoresheets during Monday post-mortem clinics, identifying missed tactics and opening pitfalls.
Will I receive an official academy completion certificate?
Yes, upon completing all curriculum milestones, passing the tactical graduation exam, and completing batch sparring requirements, students earn a verified Academy Certificate signed by GM Alexander Ivanov and their batch master.
