The Academy Origin

From a Local Chess Club to an International Academy

Chess Academy began with a single wooden demonstration board and seven eager students in a modest community center. Our founders believed that classical Soviet-era training methodologies—focused on endgame calculation, deep positional planning, and emotional stamina—could be combined with modern digital analysis tools.

Today, Chess Academy operates state-of-the-art physical training facilities equipped with digital DGT sensor boards, alongside an international online platform serving students from over 28 countries.

Pedagogical Framework

Our Four-Tier Teaching Methodology

Rather than rote memorization of moves, we instill the underlying positional logic behind every square.

01

Tactical Pattern Fluency

Drilling pins, skewers, deflections, and smothered mates until piece interaction becomes second nature.

02

Endgame Technique First

Following Capablanca's principle: mastering simplified positions with kings and pawns before complex middlegames.

03

Prophylactic Thinking

Constantly asking: "What is my opponent planning?" Neutralizing counterplay before initiating aggressive attacks.

04

Game Notation & Post-Mortem

Every game is recorded, annotated, and analyzed with your mentor to diagnose the root cause of every tactical blunder.
