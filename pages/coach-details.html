Professional Biography

Alexander earned his Grandmaster title in 2004 following a stunning performance at the Hastings International Chess Congress. Over his 20+ year competitive career, he has defeated numerous top-50 Grandmasters and represented his national squad at three Chess Olympiads.

In 2011, he dedicated his career full-time to coaching, creating a scientific curriculum that strips away guesswork and teaches pupils how to formulate decisive strategic plans regardless of the opening encountered.
