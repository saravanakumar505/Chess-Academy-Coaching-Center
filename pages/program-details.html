Program Overview

Most chess players plateau around 1200-1400 Elo because they rely on shallow one-move threats and random opening moves. This course breaks through that barrier by teaching you how to evaluate pawn structures, seize central files, spot prophylactic danger, and execute classical plans.

Every class is led live by FIDE Grandmasters and International Masters. You will receive weekly homework assignments on interactive digital boards, personalized game analysis, and sparring practice with peers in your rating bracket.

Skills You Will Develop

Opening Repertoire Mastery

Cohesive response files for White (1.e4/1.d4) and Black.

Multi-Move Tactical Vision

Spotting combinations 3-4 moves deep before initiating trades.

Positional Pawn Structures

Targeting isolated pawns, backward pawns, and outposts.

Tournament Clock Discipline

Managing the clock across 90+30 and rapid formats without panic.

Course Curriculum (12 Weeks)

  • Controlling central outposts and diagonal space.
  • The Italian Game: Giuoco Piano & Evans Gambit concepts.
  • White repertoire against the Sicilian Defense (Open Sicilian vs Alapin).
  • The resilient Caro-Kann Defense (Advance and Classical variations).
  • Nimzo-Indian & Queen's Indian harmonious piece play.
  • Navigating early tactical gambits and surprise novelties.
  • Opposite side castling attacks: pawn storms and rook lifts.
  • Carlsen's prophylactic squeeze: preventing enemy counterplay.
  • The Greek Gift sacrifice (Bxh7+) calculation criteria.
  • Lucena Position: Building the bridge technique.
  • Philidor Position: Third-rank defensive barrier.
  • Full classical tournament simulation with grandmaster review.
