Tournament Overview & Eligibility

The Autumn Grandmaster Classical Open is an official FIDE-rated competition conducted under standard FIDE Laws of Chess. The tournament will be paired using the official Swiss-Manager software with electronic live broadcast for the top 10 boards.

Eligibility Criteria:

  • Active FIDE ID or National Federation membership.
  • Open to players with published ratings between 1200 and 2600. Unrated players accepted upon placement review.
  • Grandmasters (GM) and Woman Grandmasters (WGM) receive complimentary entry upon registration prior to Nov 20.

Rules, Regulations & Fair Play

• Touch-Move Rule: Strictly enforced across all boards as per FIDE guidelines Article 4.

• Anti-Cheating Policy: No mobile phones or wearable smartwatches are permitted in the playing venue. Electronic wand screening will be active.

• Default Time: 30 minutes after scheduled round start.

• Tiebreak Criteria: Direct Encounter, Buchholz Cut-1, Buchholz Full, Sonneborn-Berger.
